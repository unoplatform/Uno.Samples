# Extra assets

These files exist so the app ships fonts and images of its own, beyond the icon and splash
inputs Uno.Resizetizer generates from. Nothing references them at runtime: they are here to be
listed by asset pickers and by anything else that reports what the application packages.

## Fonts

`Fonts/Montserrat-*.ttf` — Montserrat, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The same
files are already used by the `UnoSQLiteOneDriveInvoiceSample` sample in this repository.

## Images

`Images/*.png` — placeholder artwork generated for this sample: flat shapes on a coffee palette,
not photographs. Replace them with real imagery if this app ever needs to display any.
